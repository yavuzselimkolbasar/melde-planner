# ADR-0005: The app is called Meldo

## Decision
The owner chose direction A from the brand canvas (https://claude.ai/artifact/9DvrHv5kGnNNTkz9w1KHDe):
- **Name:** Meldo. Tagline: "Zeig auf. Wachs mit."
- **Mascot:** Meldo, a cheerful raised hand (orange #FF9A4D) with a face. It waves when you melden and becomes a fist with a flame on streaks.
- **Icon:** a white raised hand on indigo #3B3FD8 with a yellow spark (#FFD447), plus dark and tinted variants.
- **Palette:** indigo #3B3FD8 (accent), light indigo #8D90FF (dark-mode accent), orange #FF9A4D, yellow #FFD447, ink #14152A.

## Consequences
- The display name, icon, accent colour and empty states move to Meldo. The repo and bundle id can stay as they are.
- The mascot never implies grades (glossary rule): it celebrates participation, not marks.
